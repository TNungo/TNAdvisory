# TN Advisory web mockup design direction

## Approach 1: Institutional White Space
Very Brief Intro: A refined advisory site built around white space, navy typography, champagne-gold rules, and editorial hierarchy. It should feel closer to a serious strategy firm or development-finance institution than a technology startup.
Probability: 0.04

## Approach 2: Continental Systems
Very Brief Intro: A warm, map-informed African growth and trade aesthetic using charcoal, parchment, copper and carefully framed documentary imagery. It would emphasize regional context and cross-border connection.
Probability: 0.07

## Approach 3: Midnight Intelligence
Very Brief Intro: A dark, high-contrast advisory environment with restrained electric accents, used to signal technology, data and future-facing transformation.
Probability: 0.02

## Chosen approach: Institutional White Space

### Design Movement
Contemporary institutional editorial design, drawing from annual reports, development-finance institutions, and senior strategy consultancies rather than startup landing pages.

### Core Principles
1. Clarity before decoration: every section must answer what TN Advisory does, for whom, and why it is credible.
2. Strategic restraint: navy is dominant, champagne gold is a signal color, and white space carries authority.
3. Integrated capability: technology and software are presented as delivery capability within a broader strategy, trade and development-finance practice.
4. Evidence over theatre: use the real profile, real work and NeuroAccess SA as a selected social-impact platform example; do not invent clients, outcomes or testimonials.

### Color Philosophy
Deep navy communicates institutional trust and analytical seriousness. Champagne gold is used sparingly as a marker of value, decision and emphasis—not as a luxury decoration. Warm white creates the feeling of a considered executive document.

### Layout Paradigm
Asymmetric editorial layouts with a strong left reading rail, large margins and occasional right-side evidence panels. Avoid generic centered hero sections and repetitive three-card grids.

### Signature Elements
A thin champagne-gold rule; large serif insight headlines paired with precise sans-serif body copy; compact metadata labels in uppercase with generous tracking.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel deliberate and useful: clear navigation, subtle hover emphasis, readable anchor links, and no gratuitous motion. Every CTA should move a serious visitor toward a conversation, capability view, or selected work example.

### Animation
Use only short fade/translate reveals for section entrances, with reduced-motion support. Avoid looping motion, parallax and attention-seeking effects.

### Typography System
Use a refined serif for major insight headlines and a neutral sans-serif for navigation, metadata and body copy. Suggested pairing: Cormorant Garamond or Libre Baskerville with IBM Plex Sans or Source Sans 3.

### Brand Essence
TN Advisory is a technology-enabled advisory practice helping decision-makers connect strategy, software, trade and development finance into practical action. Personality: analytical, grounded, consequential.

### Brand Voice
Headlines should be decisive and concise. CTAs should sound like executive next steps, not generic growth marketing. Example lines: “Turn strategic intent into investable action.” “Start a focused executive conversation.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the approved TN monogram and ADVISORY wordmark as the primary mark, with the motto as a supporting line rather than a headline lockup everywhere.

### Signature Brand Color
Deep TN Navy: #102B50.

## Website positioning decision

TN Advisory should be presented as the advisory and technology-enabled strategy practice. NeuroAccess SA should appear as a selected social-impact platform / venture example with a clear external link, not as the whole TN Advisory identity. The website should foreground technology-led strategy, trade, transformation and development finance, grounded in the user's actual profile in trade finance, digital transformation, computer science, software development and development finance.
